# Hosted CSV results on Online Retail

**All six planned phases passed on the first and only hosted attempt.** On November 15, 2026, GitHub-hosted Ubuntu 24.04 ran Chrome over public HTTPS against ProofFlow. Chrome uploaded 642 UCI Online Retail transaction lines, created and downloaded checked reports, rejected an old-tab download after the FX correction, and downloaded corrected reports after refresh. Reload restored the corrected reports and their evidence. [Workflow run, private repository access required](https://github.com/saivedant169/proofflow/actions/runs/43758286967) · [Sanitized receipt](../results/real-partition-v1/receipt.json).

The fixture contains every eligible positive line from January 16, 2011, across 25 invoices. The deterministic scan selected the first unreserved date that fit the 1,000-row importer limit. Compact v3 previously counted that date in a whole-month profile. Its January prepared inputs ended January 9. Every eligible January 16 line is from the United Kingdom, so the company and regional values coincide. The 28,429-byte upload sums to **£7,242.06** in the source extract. The CSV supplies no refund or support observations. GBP/USD **1.25 → 1.30** is an authored correction on the transaction inputs. The independent Decimal oracle and the observed results agree to the cent. Half-even and half-up rounding agree on these two amounts.
